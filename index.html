ICHORFALL

ALIEN SWARM SHOOTER · 18+

A vertical arcade shooter for iPhone, rated 18+. Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to christian.effner@googlemail.com – usually answered within a few days.

How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to fly – your ship follows your finger and its guns fire on their own. Let enemy shots pass close by (grazing) to charge Overdrive, chain kills for a multiplier, and use a thermobaric charge to clear the screen: tap the bomb button or touch with a second finger. Each of the eight zones ends with a boss. Spend the scrap you collect in the Hangar to upgrade your gunship.

Questions

Can I turn the gore down?

Yes. On first launch you choose the gore level, and you can change it any time in Settings → Gore (Full, Reduced or Off) and Blood (crimson or green). It is purely cosmetic: scores and difficulty never change. Screen shake and flashes can be turned off there too.

I don't hear any sound.

Ichorfall follows your iPhone's silent mode and plays alongside your own music. Switch silent mode off (Ring/Silent switch or Action button) and check the volume. Sound effects and music can also be turned off in the game's settings.

How do I restore my purchases on a new iPhone?

Open Ships & boosts from the main menu and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought them with. With Family Sharing, family members get them too.

What can I buy?

Everything you need to play is free: the whole campaign, Hard mode, the Daily Run and Endless. Optional one-time purchases: the ships Wraith (railgun) and Juggernaut (flak spread), each with a free test flight first, the Scrap Booster (double scrap from every run), or all three together as Ichorfall Deluxe. No ads, no subscriptions, no loot boxes.

How do I move my progress to another device?

If iCloud is turned on for your Apple ID, Ichorfall syncs your progress between your devices automatically. Leaderboards and achievements are kept by Game Center.

When does a new Daily Run start?

Every day at midnight UTC (1:00 in Germany in winter, 2:00 in summer). Everyone plays the same run that day, with a new modifier.

Does Ichorfall collect data or show ads?

No. No ads, no tracking, no accounts. See the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
