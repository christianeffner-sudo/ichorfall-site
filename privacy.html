ICHORFALL

Privacy Policy · last updated 20 November 2026

Short version: Ichorfall does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only services involved are Apple's own: Game Center, iCloud and the App Store.

Who is responsible

Ichorfall is made by Christian Effner, Breitlacherstraße 86, 60489 Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Contact: christian.effner@googlemail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (zones, stars, scrap, upgrades, ships and paint jobs), Daily Run results, missions and settings are saved on your iPhone. They are deleted when you delete the app.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Ichorfall keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud key-value storage so it can sync between your devices. This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account; we have no access to it. You can turn this off in the iOS Settings app under your name → iCloud.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center and shown on its leaderboards under your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple; we cannot see your Apple ID or contact details. You can sign out of Game Center in the iOS Settings app at any time.

Purchases

The optional purchases (Wraith, Juggernaut, Scrap Booster and Ichorfall Deluxe) are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID.

Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center, iCloud and the App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy.

No third parties

Ichorfall contains no third-party libraries, analytics or advertising tools and has no servers of its own. It uses the internet only through Apple's Game Center, iCloud and App Store services built into iOS.

Children

Ichorfall is rated 18+ and not meant for children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Since we process no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you have questions, write to christian.effner@googlemail.com.

Changes

If Ichorfall ever starts processing data, this page will be updated before that version is released.
